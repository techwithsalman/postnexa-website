- **768px (Tablet Portrait):** Navigation smoothly transitions to a mobile hamburger drawer; 2-column split heroes stack gracefully with media previews retaining full aspect ratio.
- **430px, 390px, 375px & 320px (Mobile Devices):** Full width cards with `16px` outer padding; tap targets minimum `44px x 44px`; all font sizes legible down to 320px without horizontal scrollbars (`overflow-x: hidden`).
