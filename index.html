Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About PostNexa

Learn about our technology stack, supported platforms, and product evolution.

PostNexa is an AI-powered social media management and automation platform developed by Tech With Salman. Built for high-performance publishing speed and reliability, it allows creators, businesses, and teams to schedule posts, manage accounts, and streamline multi-channel workflows.

PostNexa connects with Facebook Pages, Instagram (Feed & Reels), YouTube, and TikTok via official developer APIs. Additional platform connections including LinkedIn, Threads, and Pinterest are in active development.

PostNexa uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0 authentication directly with social platforms. We never store your raw social media passwords. Access tokens are encrypted and managed securely to perform authorized publishing tasks on your behalf.

The web application is accessible at app.techwithsalman.online. You can register an account, connect your authorized channels, and start scheduling immediately.
