TikTok Video Size, Format & Upload Guide: 2026 Specs, Bitrates & Safe Zones

Complete technical guide to TikTok video dimensions, aspect ratios, file size limits, bitrates, and interface safe zones. Avoid cropping and compression errors.

By Tech With Salman • 10 min read • Verified 2026 Strategy

Quick Spec Checklist:

For crystal-clear TikTok playback: 1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16 vertical), MP4 or MOV, H.264 video codec, 30 or 60 fps, and a target bitrate of 15-20 Mbps. Check your media before scheduling using our free Media Size Checker.

Official 2026 TikTok Video Specifications

TikTok is an immersive, mobile-first video platform. Uploading a video that fails to match its preferred encoding standards causes TikTok's ingestion servers to re-compress your video aggressively, turning sharp footage into blurry, pixelated playback.

Parameter Recommended Value Supported Range / Notes
Resolution 1080 × 1920 px (Full HD) 720p minimum; 4K will be downsampled to 1080p
Aspect Ratio 9:16 (Vertical) Horizontal 16:9 will be padded with black bars
Container Format .MP4 or .MOV AVI, MPEG, and WebM supported on desktop web
Video Codec H.264 (AVC) or H.265 (HEVC) H.264 provides maximum playback compatibility
Audio Codec AAC Stereo (128 kbps+) 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz sample rate
Frame Rate 30 fps or 60 fps Consistent frame rate recommended
Max File Size (iOS) 287.6 MB Direct in-app mobile upload limit
Max File Size (Android) 72 MB Direct in-app mobile upload limit
Max File Size (Web / API) Up to 1 GB Supported via PostNexa and web upload interface

Navigating TikTok's Mobile Interface Safe Zones

One of the most common mistakes creators make is positioning critical text, prices, or facial reactions in areas obscured by TikTok's mobile UI overlays:

  • Right-Side Action Buttons (Like, Comment, Save, Share): Reserve an unobstructed margin of at least 120 pixels along the entire right edge.
  • Bottom Captions & Audio Ticker: The bottom 350 to 400 pixels are dynamically covered by your account handle, multi-line caption, sound title, and search bar. Never place subtitles or key graphics at the very bottom.
  • Top Navigation Tabs: The top 150 pixels display the "Following", "Friends", and "For You" switcher tabs alongside the search icon. Keep graphics slightly below this header line.

Recommended Export Settings in Video Editors

Adobe Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve Settings:

  • Format: H.264
  • Preset: Custom (Match Source - 1080×1920)
  • Bitrate Encoding: VBR, 1 Pass
  • Target Bitrate: 16 Mbps (Maximum Bitrate: 20 Mbps)
  • Render at Maximum Depth: Enabled

CapCut Mobile / Desktop Settings:

  • Resolution: 1080p
  • Frame Rate: 30 fps (or 60 fps for fast sports/action)
  • Code Rate (Bitrate): Higher or Recommended
  • Smart HDR: Disabled (unless intentionally color graded, as HDR can cause brightness shifts on SDR devices)

How to Ensure Maximum Video Quality on TikTok

  1. Enable HD Uploads in App Settings: Before publishing in the TikTok app, tap "More options" and make sure "Allow high-quality uploads" is toggled ON.
  2. Avoid In-App Compressed Re-Saves: Don't download a video from one platform with heavy compression and re-upload it to another. If you need clean creator backup files, inspect your originals with our free Social Media Video Downloader.
  3. Use Direct API Scheduling: Uploading high-bitrate files via desktop web or PostNexa's Direct Publishing Engine bypasses the aggressive mobile compression pipeline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upload 4K video to TikTok?

You can upload a 4K file, but TikTok will automatically downsample and compress it to 1080p on its servers. We recommend exporting directly in 1080×1920 from your editing software so that you maintain total control over sharpening and bitrate compression.

What is the maximum duration for a TikTok video in 2026?

TikTok supports video durations up to 10 minutes for standard uploads and up to 60 minutes for select creator pilot programs. However, the highest average completion rates occur in the 25 to 60-second range.
