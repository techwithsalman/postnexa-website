Instagram Reels Size & Dimensions Guide: Resolution, Safe Zones & Aspect Ratios

Master Instagram Reels dimensions in 2026. Detailed safe zone guides for 9:16 full-screen, 4:5 feed preview, and 1:1 profile grid cropping, with export presets.

By Tech With Salman • 10 min read • Verified 2026 Strategy

The 3-in-1 Ratio Rule:

Instagram Reels must be rendered in 9:16 (1080×1920) for full-screen playback, but they are cropped to 4:5 (1080×1350) in the main feed and 1:1 (1080×1080) on your profile grid. Learn how to design within the safe center zone below.

The Triple Crop Challenge of Instagram Reels

Designing a high-performing Instagram Reel is technically more complex than TikTok because Instagram displays your video in three completely different aspect ratios across the app:

  • 1. Full-Screen Reels Tab (9:16 • 1080×1920 px): When users tap into the dedicated Reels tab, the video displays edge-to-edge.
  • 2. Main Feed Scroll (4:5 • 1080×1350 px): When your Reel appears on your followers' standard home feed, Instagram crops roughly 285 pixels off the top and 285 pixels off the bottom. If you place your text hook at the very top, home feed viewers will see it cut in half!
  • 3. Profile Grid Thumbnail (1:1 • 1080×1080 px): On your profile page, your Reel displays as a perfect square unless a custom cover is assigned.

Instagram Reels Safe Zone Blueprint

Center 1080 × 1350 Safe Canvas

To guarantee that your Reel looks visually stunning in both the full-screen view and the main feed scroll, keep all critical elements (captions, headlines, product shots, faces) strictly within the center 1080 × 1350 box.

  • Top Margin to avoid: Top 285 pixels (cropped in home feed)
  • Bottom Margin to avoid: Bottom 285 pixels (cropped in home feed and covered by profile handle + audio title)
  • Right Side Margin to avoid: Right 120 pixels (covered by Like, Comment, and Share buttons)

Reels Cover Photo: Dimensions & Best Practices

Your Reel cover photo determines click-through rates from your profile grid and Explore page recommendations:

  • Cover Dimensions: 1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16).
  • Grid Safe Area: Center 1080 × 1080 pixels. Make sure your title text sits dead-center so it remains legible on your profile grid.
  • File Format: High-resolution PNG or JPG under 4MB.

You can quickly check your cover art aspect ratio using our free Social Media Image Size Checker.

Optimal Export Settings for Instagram Reels

Instagram's mobile app is notorious for compressing high-bitrate files. Exporting at excessively high bitrates (e.g., 50+ Mbps) actually triggers heavier compression than exporting at an optimized bitrate:

  • Resolution: 1080 × 1920 px (Do not upload 4K)
  • Frame Rate: 30 fps (or 60 fps for dynamic movement)
  • Codec: H.264, Main or High Profile
  • Bitrate: Constant Bitrate (CBR) or VBR 2-Pass at 12 to 15 Mbps
  • Audio: AAC, 128 kbps, 48 kHz stereo

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I upload a horizontal 16:9 video as a Reel?

Instagram will fit the horizontal video inside the 9:16 frame by placing large black or blurred padding bars above and below your clip. This drastically lowers watch time and engagement. Always format your video vertically for Reels.

What is the maximum duration for an Instagram Reel in 2026?

Instagram supports Reels up to 90 seconds (1 minute 30 seconds) through standard mobile and API publishing. Videos between 15 and 45 seconds typically yield the highest average completion rates.
