Creator Archiving Guides

Platform-by-Platform Saving Workflows

Learn how PostNexa inspects each network and the fastest official methods to export high-resolution MP4 files you own.

How to Save Your Own TikTok Videos

TikTok

When repurposing your short-form clips for cross-platform scheduling, paste any TikTok link (tiktok.com/@user/video/... or vm.tiktok.com/...) to verify its title, creator handle, and embed preview.

  • Automatically expands shortened vm.tiktok.com and vt.tiktok.com mobile share links.
  • Fetches official creator attribution, video title, and cover thumbnail via TikTok's oEmbed endpoint.

Saving Original MP4 Files: Connect an authorized TikTok Display API token (TIKTOK_ACCESS_TOKEN) for direct server downloads, or tap Share → Save video on your own post in the TikTok app.

How to Save Your Own Instagram Reels

Instagram

Paste any Instagram Reel (instagram.com/reel/...), feed video (/p/...), or IGTV link (/tv/...) to validate the media shortcode and load an interactive embedded preview.

  • Instant shortcode extraction and normalized URL verification for Reels and video posts.
  • Loads the official Instagram embedded player for rapid visual review before repurposing.

Owner Authorization: Meta restricts unauthenticated scraping. Download your own published Reels via the Instagram mobile app (Menu → Your Activity → Download) or connect an Instagram Graph API media_url token.

How to Download Your Facebook Videos

Facebook

PostNexa detects standard Facebook Watch links (facebook.com/watch/?v=...), Page videos, Facebook Reels (facebook.com/reel/...), and fb.watch/ short URLs.

  • Resolves redirected fb.watch links and extracts the canonical Facebook video identifier.
  • Displays OpenGraph metadata and an interactive Facebook Watch embed player when public.

Meta Business Suite & Graph API: Page admins can export HD MP4 masters directly inside Meta Business Suite → Content → Published, or by configuring a Page-scoped FACEBOOK_ACCESS_TOKEN.

YouTube Download Options and Limitations

YouTube

Paste any youtube.com/watch?v=..., youtube.com/shorts/..., or youtu.be/... link to immediately inspect live video metadata, channel attribution, and high-res thumbnails.

  • Real-time YouTube oEmbed inspection with 16:9 high-definition thumbnail and embed playback.
  • Full compliance with YouTube Terms of Service — zero unauthorized stream cipher scraping.

YouTube Studio Export: To download MP4 files of videos you own, open YouTube Studio → Content → click the three-dot menu next to your video → select Download.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how PostNexa's Social Media Video Downloader inspects links and handles media permissions.

PostNexa automatically detects video links from TikTok (tiktok.com, vm.tiktok.com, vt.tiktok.com), Instagram (/reel/, /p/, /tv/), Facebook (/reel/, /watch/, /videos/, fb.watch), and YouTube (youtube.com/watch, youtube.com/shorts/, youtu.be/), as well as authorized first-party PostNexa MP4 assets.

Major social platforms (including YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok) enforce strict API policies and Terms of Service that prohibit unauthenticated third-party scraping of raw video streams. Rather than using unreliable scraper endpoints or showing fake download buttons, PostNexa retrieves documented public metadata (such as oEmbed titles, thumbnails, and official embeds) and only enables direct MP4 downloads when a verified, permitted video file stream is available.
