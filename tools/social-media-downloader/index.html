Step-by-Step Workflow

How to Download Social Media Videos

Inspect, verify, and save your own or authorized social media videos in three straightforward steps.

Step 1

Copy Your Video Link

Open TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube, locate the video you created or have permission to archive, and copy its share URL to your clipboard.

Step 2

Automatic Platform Detection

Paste the link into PostNexa's universal input bar. Our engine automatically identifies the source platform, resolves short links, and checks permitted media access.

Step 3

Preview & Save Permitted Media

Review the video metadata, thumbnail, and preview player. Where an authorized direct MP4 stream is permitted, click Download MP4 to save the verified video file.

How to Save Your Own TikTok Videos

When repurposing your short-form clips for cross-platform scheduling inside PostNexa, you can inspect any TikTok URL (tiktok.com/@username/video/... or vm.tiktok.com/...) to verify its title, creator handle, and embed preview.

Saving Original MP4 Files: For unauthenticated public links, TikTok's oEmbed API provides metadata and preview players. To download raw MP4 files directly, connect an authorized TikTok Display API token (TIKTOK_ACCESS_TOKEN) or tap Share → Save video on your own post inside the TikTok mobile app.

How to Save Your Own Instagram Reels

Paste any Instagram Reel (instagram.com/reel/...), feed video (/p/...), or IGTV link (/tv/...) into the downloader to validate the shortcode and load an official embedded preview.

Owner Authorization: Meta restricts unauthenticated third-party scraping of Instagram Reels. Creators can download their own published Reels via the Instagram mobile app (Menu → Your Activity → Archived / Download) or through an authenticated Instagram Graph API media_url integration.

How to Download Your Facebook Videos

PostNexa detects standard Facebook Watch links (facebook.com/watch/?v=...), Page videos (facebook.com/pagename/videos/...), Facebook Reels (facebook.com/reel/...), and fb.watch/ short URLs.

Meta Business Suite & Graph API: Page administrators can download high-definition MP4 copies of their uploaded videos through Meta Business Suite → Content → Published, or by configuring a Page-scoped FACEBOOK_ACCESS_TOKEN on the backend worker.

YouTube Download Options and Limitations

Paste any youtube.com/watch?v=..., youtube.com/shorts/..., or youtu.be/... link to immediately fetch real-time video metadata, channel attribution, high-resolution thumbnails, and an official embedded preview via YouTube's documented oEmbed API.

YouTube Terms of Service Compliance: PostNexa does not perform unauthorized third-party stream extraction from YouTube. To download MP4 files of videos you own, sign in to YouTube Studio, open Content, click the three-dot menu next to your video, and select Download.
