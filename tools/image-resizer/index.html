Frequently Asked Questions

Will resizing reduce the quality of my image?

Scaling down (downsampling) preserves crisp details using high-quality bicubic interpolation. Enlarging images by more than 200% may cause minor pixelation depending on the original source resolution.

Can I resize multiple images at once?

You can resize individual images consecutively with instant downloads.

What happens if I unlock the aspect ratio?

Unlocking the aspect ratio allows you to set width and height independently, which is useful when matching strict square or banner aspect requirements.
