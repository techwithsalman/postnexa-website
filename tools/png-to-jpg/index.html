Why Convert PNG to JPG?

PNG files can be five to ten times larger than JPEGs. Converting to JPG makes images easier to attach to emails, upload to marketplace listings, and store on mobile devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to transparent areas in the PNG?

Because JPEG does not support transparency, transparent areas are seamlessly rendered with a clean white background.

How much smaller will the JPG file be?

Typically, converting photographic or complex PNGs to JPG reduces file size by 60% to 85%.

Is my data secure?
