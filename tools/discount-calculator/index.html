Frequently Asked Questions

How is sales tax calculated on discounted items?

In most tax jurisdictions, sales tax applies to the final discounted purchase price rather than the original MSRP.

Can I calculate stacking discounts?

Calculate the first discount, then apply the second percentage against the discounted price.

Does this support international currencies?

Yes. The mathematical ratios work identically regardless of whether you are computing in USD, EUR, GBP, or CAD.
