Free Social Media Content Calendar Generator

Build a balanced 7-day sprint or 30-day multi-channel editorial schedule. Edit hooks and formats directly in your browser and download a spreadsheet ready to import into your queue.

Configure Your Editorial Schedule

Select your industry, active social channels, and target cadence. We will structure a balanced editorial mix balancing education, entertainment, social proof, and promotion.

The Science of Building a Balanced Social Media Calendar

Most brands fail on social media because they treat posting as a daily scramble rather than an operational pipeline. When you plan ahead using proven content pillars, you eliminate stress and guarantee consistent audience retention.

How to Import Your CSV Plan into PostNexa

  1. Click the Download CSV Plan button above to save your customized spreadsheet.
  2. Log in to your PostNexa account at app.postnexa.world.
  3. Navigate to the Bulk Scheduler module in your workspace sidebar.
  4. Upload your CSV file, review scheduled timestamps, attach your creative media, and click Queue All Posts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What formats does the content calendar generator export?

You can export your complete structured schedule as a ready-to-use CSV spreadsheet (compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, Notion) or copy the formatted plan to your clipboard.

How do content pillars improve publishing consistency?

Organizing around 3-5 core themes (e.g. Educational, Proof, Behind-the-Scenes, Product) prevents creator fatigue and ensures a balanced narrative that algorithmically builds audience trust.

Can I import this CSV into PostNexa scheduling software?

Yes! The CSV output follows PostNexa’s Bulk CSV Scheduling format, allowing you to queue 30 days of posts across 6 networks in minutes.
