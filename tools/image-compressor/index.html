How to Compress Images with Zero Quality Loss

Optimizing high-resolution images is crucial for website speed, email deliverability, and Core Web Vitals performance. PostNexa provides browser-native compression that preserves visual clarity while slashing payload size.

1

Upload Image

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my uploaded image sent to an external server?

No. PostNexa’s image compressor executes 100% inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your pictures never leave your computer.

What is the recommended compression percentage for websites?

For web publishing and blog thumbnails, a quality setting between 70% and 80% typically reduces file size by 60% to 80% without any perceptible loss in sharpness.

Can I compress PNG images with transparent backgrounds?

Yes. PNG transparency is fully maintained throughout the compression and download workflow.
