Quick Reference: 2026 Social Media Aspect Ratio Guidelines

Using the wrong aspect ratio causes platforms to either automatically crop important details or add unsightly letterbox padding. Bookmark these core rules for high-engagement publishing:

Placement Recommended Size Aspect Ratio Strategic Benefit
Instagram Feed Post 1080 × 1350 px 4:5 (Portrait) Fills maximum vertical screen height on iPhones and Androids
Reels & TikTok Videos 1080 × 1920 px 9:16 (Vertical) Full screen immersive view; keep text inside safe center zone
YouTube Thumbnail 1280 × 720 px 16:9 (Landscape) Standard HD thumbnail; stay under 2MB file limit
LinkedIn Feed Post 1200 × 627 px 1.91:1 (Landscape) Clean display on company feeds without thumbnail clipping
