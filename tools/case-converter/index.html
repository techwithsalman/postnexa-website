Understanding Different Text Casing Formats

Proper casing ensures professional presentation across marketing copy, headline typography, and software development.

1

Title Case

Capitalizes the first letter of every major word. Standard for headlines, blog titles, and menu items.

2

Sentence Case

Capitalizes only the first word of each sentence and proper nouns. Natural for body copy and social captions.

3

Kebab-Case (Slug)

Converts spaces into hyphens and lowercases all characters. Essential for clean SEO URLs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Title Case preserve acronyms?

Acronyms entered in uppercase will be formatted with initial capital letters. Double-check specialized brand names after conversion.

Can I convert code identifiers to camelCase?

Yes. The camelCase option collapses spaces and capitalizes subsequent word initials.

Is there a limit on text length?

No limit. You can format short phrases or full articles.
